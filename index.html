My Story

Understanding the complexity of the human experience.

At the heart of everything I do is a deep desire to understand the full complexity of human experience, and to tell stories that shed light on why we do what we do.

My journey began in software engineering, building integrated technical systems. While I enjoyed the architectural rigor, I found myself continually drawn to the human element behind the screen—how people navigate choices, form habits, and make sense of their lives.

That curiosity led me to an MSc in Behavioral Economics and 5+ years leading behavioral strategy for global brands. Whether diagnosing why patients drop off life-saving treatment regimens or why innovative product launches stall, I specialize in unpicking non-conscious friction points—habits, social pressure, and cognitive fatigue—and building actionable strategic roadmaps.

For me, storytelling takes many forms. Beyond commercial strategy, I hosted a corporate behavioral science podcast—translating dense academic insights into engaging audio—and in my free time, I write original song lyrics and compose piano music. Whether through a song, a podcast episode, or a product strategy deck, my passion lies in uncovering authentic human insights and translating them into meaningful impact.

Today, I help innovative tech and digital health companies build intuitive products, reduce user friction, and meet people's conscious and non-conscious needs.

Digital Health & Product Strategy Anonymized Client Study

Optimizing Adherence by Reducing Cognitive Burden & Stigma in Long-Acting Care

The Challenge

Patients facing stigmatized chronic conditions experienced significant drop-off despite available high-efficacy treatments.

Behavioral Diagnostic

Uncovered two primary psychological barriers: fear of involuntary disclosure and the daily cognitive burden/reminder of illness.

Strategic Impact

Reframed product positioning from physical convenience to mental freedom, proving patients willingly trade short-term pain for lower dosing frequency.

Commercial Strategy & Brand Positioning Anonymized Client Study

Leveraging Behavioral Science to Outposition Larger Competitors

The Challenge

A regional specialized manufacturer needed a positioning strategy to compete against massive, resource-rich global competitors.

Behavioral Diagnostic

Analyzed buyer psychology to reveal that decision-makers prioritized personal rapport, responsiveness, and specialized focus over sheer company scale.

Strategic Impact

Leveraged the Specialization Effect and Country-of-Origin trust cues to reframe smaller size as an advantage, equipping sales teams with an personalized trust narrative.

Behavior Change & Messaging Optimization Anonymized Client Study

Reframing Behavioral Drivers to Shift Prescribing & Adoption Patterns

The Challenge

Existing campaigns relied on loss aversion and system-cost guilt, which failed to motivate clinical decision-makers to switch behaviors and often backfired.

Behavioral Diagnostic

Diagnosed high psychological distance: macro system costs felt impersonal and irrelevant, whereas clinicians were intrinsically focused on immediate patient outcomes.

Strategic Impact

Replaced guilt messaging with Descriptive Social Proof and peer validation, establishing new prescribing habits as the modern clinical standard.
