My Story

Jeremy Koloski

Behavioral Scientist & Product Strategist

Understanding the full complexity of human experience.

At the heart of everything I do is a deep desire to uncover why people make the choices they do—and to build products and stories that meet their real needs.

My journey began in software engineering, designing integrated technical systems. While I loved structural problem-solving, I found myself drawn to the human element behind the screen—how users navigate choices, form habits, and overcome friction.

Driven by this curiosity, I earned an MSc in Behavioral Economics and spent over 5 years leading behavioral strategy for global brands. Whether diagnosing why patients drop off life-saving treatment regimens or why innovative product launches stall, I specialize in unpicking non-conscious friction points—habits, cognitive fatigue, and social pressure—and translating them into actionable product roadmaps.

For me, storytelling takes many forms. Beyond diagnostic research, I hosted a corporate behavioral science podcast and write original song lyrics and piano music. Whether through an audio narrative, a song, or an executive product deck, my passion lies in uncovering authentic human insights and driving meaningful impact.

A few highlights from the hundreds of projects I've worked on over the past few years. NDA-compliant summaries showcasing behavioral diagnostics, strategic reframing, and product impact.

Digital Health & Product Strategy Anonymized Client Study

Optimizing Adherence by Reducing Cognitive Burden & Stigma in Long-Acting Care

The Challenge

Patients facing stigmatized chronic conditions experienced significant drop-off despite available high-efficacy treatments.

Behavioral Diagnostic

Uncovered two primary psychological barriers: fear of involuntary disclosure and the daily cognitive burden/reminder of illness.

Strategic Impact

Reframed product positioning from physical convenience to mental freedom, proving patients willingly trade short-term pain for lower dosing frequency.

Commercial Strategy & Brand Positioning Anonymized Client Study

Leveraging Behavioral Economics to Outposition Market Incumbents

The Challenge

A regional specialized manufacturer needed a positioning strategy to compete against massive, resource-rich global incumbents.

Behavioral Diagnostic

Analyzed buyer psychology to reveal that decision-makers prioritized personal rapport, responsiveness, and specialized focus over sheer company scale.

Strategic Impact

Leveraged the Specialization Effect and Country-of-Origin trust cues to reframe size as nimbleness, equipping sales teams with an unbeatable trust narrative.

Behavior Change & Messaging Optimization Anonymized Client Study

Reframing Behavioral Drivers to Shift Prescribing & Adoption Patterns

The Challenge

Existing campaigns relied on loss aversion and system-cost guilt, which failed to motivate clinical decision-makers to switch behaviors.

Behavioral Diagnostic

Diagnosed high psychological distance: macro system costs felt impersonal, whereas clinicians were intrinsically focused on immediate patient outcomes.

Strategic Impact

Replaced guilt messaging with Descriptive Social Proof and peer validation, establishing new prescribing habits as the modern clinical standard.
